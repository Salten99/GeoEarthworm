GeoEarthworm

Turn a spreadsheet of borehole measurements into bedrock depth and overburden thickness maps.

Upload a CSV of boreholes, and optionally a surface elevation GeoTIFF. Choose IDW, kriging or spline, then explore the result on an interactive map and download GeoTIFFs for your GIS software.

Open GeoEarthworm

The app runs on Streamlit Community Cloud. If nobody has used it for a while it takes about 30 seconds to wake up; click the wake-up button if you see one.

Source code and the Python library: github.com/Salten99/GeoEarthworm
